Privacy Policy

Template — review with a lawyer (e.g. for GDPR / CCPA / Taiwan PDPA) before launch.

What we collect

  • Account data: e-mail address, a hashed password (never the password itself), and your Google account ID if you sign in with Google.
  • Billing data: handled by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer and subscription IDs and plan status — not your card details.
  • Usage data: your watchlist, and which stock pages you opened today (to apply free-plan limits).
  • Cookies: one essential session cookie to keep you logged in and protect forms. No advertising trackers.

How we use it

To run your account, enforce plan limits, send account e-mails (confirmation, password reset, receipts, alerts you asked for) and keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal data.

Processors

Stripe (payments), Google (optional sign-in), our e-mail provider, and our hosting provider.

Your rights

You can update your password or delete your account (and its data) at any time from the account page.

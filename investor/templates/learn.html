How StockRadar works

Every stock gets four scores from 0 to 100. A score of 80 means the stock beats 80% of its sector peers on that dimension.

💎 Value — is it cheap?

  • P/E, P/FCF, EV/EBITDA, P/B — price paid per dollar of earnings, cash flow and assets. Lower is cheaper.
  • Graham Number — Benjamin Graham's maximum price: √(22.5 × EPS × book value per share).
  • DCF margin of safety — how far the price sits below a simple discounted-cash-flow value.
  • FCF & dividend yield — cash the business throws off relative to its price.

🚀 Growth — is it getting bigger?

  • 3-year revenue & EPS growth (compound annual rates).
  • Forward EPS growth & estimate revisions — what analysts expect, and whether they're raising numbers.
  • PEG — P/E divided by growth; under 1 is often called "growth at a reasonable price".
  • Gross-margin trend & Rule of 40 — growth % + free-cash-flow margin %, used for software.

🏰 Quality — is it a good business?

  • ROIC & ROE — profit earned on the money invested in the business.
  • Piotroski F-Score (0–9) — nine pass/fail tests of profitability, leverage and efficiency.
  • Altman Z-Score — bankruptcy-risk model; above 3 is safe, below 1.8 is distress.
  • Margin stability, cash conversion, dilution, debt/equity.

📈 Momentum & sentiment — is the crowd noticing?

  • Return vs the S&P 500, position in the 52-week range.
  • News tone — headlines rated positive/negative (by Claude when configured).
  • Social buzz — Reddit & StockTwits mentions vs their normal level (σ = standard deviations).
  • Insider buying — open-market purchases reported on SEC Form 4.
